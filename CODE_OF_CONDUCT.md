# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Shift Calendar Engine a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Avoid discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact or maintainer contact. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
