## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-31

### Added

- Cross-platform Flutter application foundation.
- Canonical schedule domain and dependency composition root.
- Responsive Material 3 shell and monthly roster viewer.
- Employee directory and configurable shift-template catalogs.
- Manual roster editing with preview and explicit save.
- Thai and English localization.
- Local persistence for schedule and catalog data.
- Monthly A4 PDF reporting, preview, printing, and sharing.
